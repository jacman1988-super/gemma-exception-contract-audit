Executable contract audit
ECA-6 is six related templated tasks with 36 constructed faulty variants. The weak gate accepted all 36.
The first contract gate rejected 35/36; six model repairs passed. A post-outcome v2 gate rejected 36/36
mutants, accepted 5/6 model repairs and accepted 6/6 references. The stronger gate was designed after
outcomes and is not independent validation. Local replay reproduces 102 variants and compares every
recorded case and semantic assertion.

Assistance, failures and scope
These are adaptive repair case studies. Initial and feedback attempts failed. The final Tenacity patch used
staged diagnosis and literal-function output. Packaging required explicit guidance to separate two valid
filename families into branches after earlier repairs still failed. Public commit descriptions and known
source localization were provided. This is not an autonomous or contamination-free first-attempt success
rate.
The final generated function bodies were applied without manual semantic edits, using recorded source
replacement rules and retained surrounding decorators. One Tenacity diagnosis incorrectly suggested that
0 versus 0.0 itself explained the failure; passing tests do not endorse that explanation. Failed outputs and
preflight corrections remain available in the evidence archive.
Model and reproducibility
Local model: Gemma 4 E2B QAT, Q4_0 GGUF; revision 675cff42a74c774d6cb76f76d8eacb49b48c9b93.
Runtime: llama.cpp b11429, commit d81235049. Recorded settings: context 4096, temperature 0, seed
20261016, two CPU threads, reasoning off. Model and prompt digests are checked by the release tools.
reproduce.py replays ECA without a model. replay_repairs.py verifies pinned upstream archive hashes
and reruns fixed, buggy-parent and final model variants. generate_repairs.py --plan lists the final guided
prompts and settings; fresh generation records new outputs separately and does not certify them.
FETCH_UPSTREAM.md supplies exact revisions and archive digests.
